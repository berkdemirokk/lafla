# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** [GÜNCELLE]

At Lafla ("the App", "we", "us", "our") we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, process, store, and protect your personal data when you use the Lafla iOS application. This policy complies with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), and Apple App Store privacy requirements.

Data Controller: Lafla, hello@lafla.app

## 1. Data We Collect

You can use Lafla without creating an account, in which case data is stored locally on your device only. If you choose to create an account, we process the following data:

**Account Information**
- Email address (only if you sign in with email or Apple)
- User identifier (a randomly generated ID)

**In-App Data**
- Your selected interests and learning preferences
- Lesson progress and completed units
- Experience points (XP) and streak data
- Your answers to questions and exercises, including correct/incorrect logs and response times

**Technical Data**
- Device type, OS version, app version
- Crash reports and error logs (via Sentry)
- Usage analytics (via PostHog, when enabled in the future)

**Payment Data**
- Subscription status and purchase records (processed via RevenueCat and Apple)
- Your credit card or banking details are **never seen or stored** by Lafla

## 2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely for the following purposes:

- Providing core app functionality (saving lesson progress, showing personalized content)
- Syncing your progress across devices (if you have an account)
- Managing premium subscriptions
- Detecting crashes and improving app stability
- Complying with legal obligations
- Contacting you (only for account-related communications)

We **do not** sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

## 3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR Article 6)

- **Performance of a contract:** To provide the app and manage premium subscriptions
- **Legitimate interest:** Crash analytics and product improvement
- **Consent:** For analytics tracking (we will request opt-in consent when activated)
- **Legal obligation:** For tax and accounting records

## 4. Third-Party Service Providers

Some of your data is processed by the following trusted service providers:

| Service | Purpose | Data Location |
|---|---|---|
| Supabase | Database and authentication | EU (Frankfurt) |
| Apple App Store | Payment processing | Global |

These providers act as data processors under our instructions and only access data necessary to deliver their services. We maintain Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) with each provider.

## 5. International Data Transfers

Some of our service providers are located outside Turkey and the EU, particularly in the United States. For such transfers we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and appropriate technical safeguards, in line with GDPR Chapter V and KVKK Article 9.

## 6. Data Retention

- **Active accounts:** Data is retained as long as your account is active
- **After account deletion:** Data is deleted or anonymized within **90 days** of account closure
- **Legal retention obligations:** Invoices and payment records may be retained longer to comply with tax law (typically 10 years)
- **Crash reports:** Automatically deleted after 30 days

You may request earlier deletion at any time (see Section 7).

## 7. Your Rights

Under GDPR Articles 15-22 and KVKK Article 11 you have the following rights:

- **Right of access:** Know what personal data we hold about you
- **Right to rectification:** Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Right to erasure (right to be forgotten):** Request deletion of your data
- **Right to restriction of processing:** Limit how we process your data
- **Right to data portability:** Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
- **Right to object:** Object to processing based on legitimate interest
- **Right not to be subject to automated decision-making**
- **Right to withdraw consent** at any time where processing is based on consent

To exercise these rights, write to **hello@lafla.app**. We respond within 30 days.

EU residents may lodge a complaint with their national data protection authority. Turkish residents may file complaints with the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK).

## 8. Children's Privacy

Lafla is **not intended for users under the age of 13**. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (in compliance with COPPA). Users between 13 and 18 should use the app with parental consent. If we discover that a child under 13 has created an account, we will delete it immediately. Parents who believe their child has provided data may contact us at hello@lafla.app for prompt removal.

## 9. Security Measures

We implement industry-standard measures to protect your data:

- All data in transit is encrypted with **TLS 1.2+**
- Database access is restricted via role-based access control (RBAC)
- Supabase Row Level Security (RLS) ensures users can only access their own data
- Sensitive configuration values are stored in secure environment variables
- Regular security reviews are performed

No system is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours and inform affected users without undue delay, as required by GDPR Article 33 and KVKK Article 12.

## 10. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

As a mobile app, Lafla does not use browser cookies. Local settings are stored on your device via AsyncStorage and never leave your device unless explicitly synced to your account.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will notify you of material changes through an in-app notice. The updated policy takes effect when published in the app or at **lafla.app/privacy**.
